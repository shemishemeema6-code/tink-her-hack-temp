Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my bus?

+

Simply go to the "Track Bus" page and select your route number or bus number. The system will display real-time location information, estimated arrival time, and upcoming stops.

How often is the location updated?

+

Bus locations are updated every 10-15 seconds to provide you with the most accurate real-time information. The frequency may vary depending on network conditions.

How accurate are the ETA estimates?

+

Our ETA calculations are based on real-time GPS data, current traffic conditions, and historical route data. Typically, ETAs are accurate within 2-5 minutes. Actual arrival times may vary due to unexpected traffic or road conditions.

Can I set notifications for my bus?

+

Yes! You can enable notifications to receive alerts when your bus is approaching, has arrived, or if there are any service disruptions. Notifications can be managed in your account settings.

Is my location data safe?

+

Your privacy is important to us. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Your location is only used to show you bus tracking information and is never shared with third parties without your consent.

What should I do if my bus is delayed?

+

If your bus is delayed, you'll receive an automatic notification. You can check the "Alerts & Notifications" section to see the reason for the delay and updated ETA. An alternative route may also be suggested if available.

How do I report a problem with a bus?

+

You can report issues using the "Contact Us" link at the bottom of any page. Fill out the issue report with details about the bus number, time, and nature of the problem. Our support team will respond within 24 hours.

Is Bus Tracker available offline?

+

Currently, Bus Tracker requires an internet connection to provide real-time updates. However, you can download a route schedule for offline reference. We're working on an offline mode feature.
